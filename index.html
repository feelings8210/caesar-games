🚂 Roads vs Railways (公路与铁路)

公路 (Roads): 1 step in any connected direction.
铁路 (Railways): Move straight along clear tracks. Only 工兵 (Engineer) can turn corners along connected tracks!

工兵

💣 Mines, Bombs & Camps (地雷、炸弹与行营)

地雷 (Mine): Static defense. Defeats all attackers except 工兵 or 炸弹.
炸弹 (Bomb): Mutual destruction with any piece.
行营 (Campsite): Safe haven! Pieces inside cannot be attacked.

🔄 Flip Mode Rules (翻棋模式)

Travel-friendly shared screen mode. 50 pieces shuffled face down. First reveal locks your army color. Per turn: Reveal 1 piece OR move 1 revealed piece.
