# Caesar Games Gomoku rules

Caesar Games uses **Freestyle Gomoku on a 15×15 board**.

- Stones are placed on intersections.
- Black moves first, then the players alternate.
- A stone cannot move or be removed after placement.
- The first unbroken horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line of **five or more**
  stones wins.
- There are no captures, opening restrictions, overline penalties, or forbidden
  moves.
- A completely filled board with no winning line is a draw.

This intentionally simple family variant is identified in saved games as
`gomoku-freestyle-15-v1`.
